# MATLAB Engineering Projects ⚡

Welcome to my MATLAB Engineering Projects repository!

This repository is where I practice programming and apply mathematical and engineering concepts using MATLAB. I created these projects to strengthen my understanding of **Electrical Engineering, Signals and Systems, and engineering calculations** while improving my problem-solving and coding skills.

## 📚 Projects Included

### 📈 Signals and Systems
- **Addition of Two Signals** — Adds two signals and plots the resulting signal.
- **Discrete-Time Convolution** — Demonstrates convolution of discrete-time signals.
- **Energy of a Discrete-Time Signal** — Calculates the energy of a discrete-time signal.
- **Even and Odd Signal Decomposition** — Separates a signal into its even and odd components.
- **Exponential Signal** — Generates and plots exponential signals.
- **Rectangular Pulse** — Creates and visualizes a rectangular pulse.
- **Signum Signal** — Demonstrates the signum function.
- **Unit Impulse (Delta) Signal** — Illustrates the discrete unit impulse signal.
- **Unit Ramp Signal** — Generates and plots a ramp signal.
- **Unit Step Signal** — Demonstrates the unit step function.
- **Signal Derivation and Integration** — Visualizes the differentiation and integration of a signal.
- **Signal Multiplication** — Multiplies two signals and plots the result.
- **Signal Visualization** — Explores and plots different types of signals.
- **Time Scaling** — Demonstrates how time scaling changes a signal.
- **Straight Line** — Draws and visualizes a straight line using MATLAB.

### ⚡ Electrical Engineering
- **V, I, R, P Calculator** — Calculates voltage, current, resistance, and power using basic electrical formulas.
- **Finding MMF** — Calculates magnetomotive force (MMF) in magnetic circuits.
- **Ohm's Law in a Loop** — Practices electrical calculations using a loop.
